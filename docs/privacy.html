Privacy Policy for Sankalp

Application: Sankalp | Package Name: one.Sankalp | Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Sankalp ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparency about how our application handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Sankalp collects only the minimum information necessary to provide habit transformation and digital detox features:

  • Account Information: You can use Sankalp in Guest Mode without providing any personal details. If you create an account, we store your name and email address to synchronize habits and progress across devices.
  • Habit & Streak Data: Habits you configure, completion timestamps, streak counts, and level milestones.
  • Digital Detox Data: Durations of focus sessions and user-configured blocked application packages. This data stays local on your device.
Location & GPS Notice: Sankalp does NOT collect, track, store, or share your GPS or coarse location. All location tracking features have been completely omitted from this application.

2. Android Device Permissions

Sankalp requests the following permissions strictly to enable core functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to deliver your daily morning and evening habit reminders and streak milestone alerts.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage Access): Required exclusively for the Digital Detox blocker feature to detect when a blocked distracting application is launched during an active focus timer.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display over other apps): Required to show the focus screen over restricted apps when focus mode is active.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to trigger exact scheduled reminders for daily routines.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Local SQLite Storage: All habits, logs, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • HTTPS Encryption: All communications with our cloud servers are encrypted using TLS/SSL (HTTPS).
  • No Advertisements / No Data Selling: We do not show ads, do not integrate third-party ad networks, and never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual habits, routines, or focus presets directly within the app at any time. You may also request complete deletion of your account and server records by contacting our support team.

5. Children's Privacy

Sankalp is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
