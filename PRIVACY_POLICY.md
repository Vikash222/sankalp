# Privacy Policy for Sankalp

**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Application Name:** Sankalp  
**Package Name:** `one.Sankalp`  

Welcome to **Sankalp** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our app. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Sankalp mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

1. **Account Information:**  
   - You can use Sankalp in **Guest Mode** without providing any personally identifiable information.  
   - If you choose to create an account, we may collect your name and email address to sync your habits, streaks, and achievements across devices.

2. **Habit & Routine Data:**  
   - Information about the habits you configure, check-in timestamps, streaks, and XP points.

3. **Digital Detox & Focus Data:**  
   - Duration of focus sessions and user-selected blocked applications. This information is processed strictly on your device to enforce focus lockouts and is never sold or shared.

4. **Location Data:**  
   - **We do NOT collect, access, track, or share your GPS or location data.** Location tracking features have been completely omitted from Sankalp.

---

### 2. Device Permissions Used

To deliver habit-tracking and digital detox features, Sankalp requests the following permissions:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):**  
  Used solely to deliver daily habit reminders, routine alerts, and streak notifications.

- **Usage Stats (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`):**  
  Used exclusively for the Digital Detox feature to detect when a blocked app is launched during an active focus mode.

- **Overlay Permission (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`):**  
  Used to display the "Focus Session Active" screen over restricted distracting apps during an active timer.

- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`):**  
  Used to trigger timely alarms for your scheduled morning protocols and daily habits.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage:** All your habit data, routines, and app blocking preferences are stored locally on your device in secure SQLite storage.
- **HTTPS Encryption:** When synchronizing with our backend servers, all data is transmitted securely over TLS/SSL (HTTPS).
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** We do not display third-party advertisements, do not integrate advertising SDKs, and do not sell your personal information.

---

### 4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You maintain complete ownership of your personal data:
- You can edit or delete any habit, routine, or focus preset directly within the App.
- You can request complete deletion of your account and associated server records at any time by contacting our support team.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

Sankalp is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
